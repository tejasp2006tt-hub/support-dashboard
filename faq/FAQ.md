# Frequently Asked Questions

## Account Issues

1. How do I reset my password?
   Use the password reset link on the login page.

2. Why is my account locked?
   Multiple failed login attempts may temporarily lock your account.

3. How can I update my email address?
   Visit Account Settings and update your profile information.

4. Why am I not receiving verification emails?
   Check your spam folder and verify your email address.

## Server Errors

5. What does a 500 error mean?
   An internal server error occurred.

6. What should I do during downtime?
   Check the status page for updates.

7. How can I report a service outage?
   Create a support ticket with relevant details.

## Deployment Issues

8. Why did my deployment fail?
   Review build logs and dependency versions.

9. How do I roll back a deployment?
   Use your deployment platform's rollback feature.

10. Why are environment variables not working?
    Verify configuration and restart services.

## API Errors

11. What does 401 Unauthorized mean?
    Authentication credentials are missing or invalid.

12. What causes a 403 Forbidden error?
    Access permissions are insufficient.

13. Why am I getting a 404 error?
    The requested resource was not found.

14. What causes API timeouts?
    Slow responses or network issues.

## SSL Issues

15. Why is my SSL certificate invalid?
    The certificate may have expired.

16. How do I renew SSL certificates?
    Renew through your certificate provider.

## Performance Issues

17. Why is the application slow?
    Possible causes include high CPU usage or database bottlenecks.

18. How can I improve performance?
    Optimize queries and monitor resource usage.

19. What causes high memory consumption?
    Memory leaks or excessive workloads.

20. How can I contact support?
    Submit a support ticket through the support portal.
